Lesson 1

🌱 Present Simple

نستخدم Present Simple عندما نتحدث عن العادات، الحقائق، والأشياء التي تحدث بشكل متكرر.

الفكرة الأساسية:

مع: I / You / We / They
نستعمل الفعل الأساسي.

مع: He / She / It
نغيّر شكل الفعل غالبًا بإضافة -s / -es / -ies.
Examples

I work every day.
You work every day.
We work every day.
They work every day.

He works every day.
She works every day.
It works well.
Foundation

👥 The 7 Subjects

هذه هي الضمائر الأساسية التي يجب أن تعرفيها قبل تصريف الأفعال.

I أنا
You أنت / أنتم
He هو
She هي
It هو/هي لشيء أو حيوان
We نحن
They هم / هن
⭐ أهم قاعدة في Present Simple:

I / You / We / They → work
He / She / It → works
Verb Rules

🔤 How Do Verbs Change?

عندما يكون الفاعل He / She / It، لا نضيف دائمًا حرف s بطريقة عشوائية. توجد عدة قواعد.

1️⃣ Add -S

معظم الأفعال تأخذ -s.


work → works
read → reads
play → plays
help → helps
open → opens

2️⃣ Add -ES

إذا انتهى الفعل بـ: s / sh / ch / x / o نضيف -es.


go → goes
watch → watches
wash → washes
fix → fixes
do → does

3️⃣ Change Y → IES

إذا انتهى الفعل بـ consonant + y، نحذف y ونضيف ies.


study → studies
try → tries
carry → carries
study → studies

4️⃣ Vowel + Y

إذا كان قبل y حرف متحرك، لا نحذف y. نضيف فقط s.


play → plays
enjoy → enjoys
stay → stays

5️⃣ Irregular Forms

بعض الأفعال لها شكل خاص ويجب حفظه.


have → has
be → is
do → does
⚠️ ملاحظة مهمة:
هذه التغييرات تحدث في Present Simple مع He / She / It. أما مع I / You / We / They فنستعمل الفعل الأساسي.
Lesson 2

⏳ Past Simple

نستخدم Past Simple لشيء حدث وانتهى في الماضي. غالبًا نستخدم كلمات مثل: yesterday, last week, last year, two days ago.

Regular verbs

work → worked
play → played
watch → watched
clean → cleaned
study → studied
try → tried
Irregular verbs

go → went
eat → ate
see → saw
drink → drank
write → wrote
take → took
make → made
speak → spoke
I worked yesterday.
She studied last night.
They went to university yesterday.
He ate breakfast this morning.
Lesson 3

🚀 Future with WILL

نستخدم will + base verb عندما نتحدث عن المستقبل. الميزة المهمة هنا: الفعل لا يتغير مع He / She / It.

I will work.
You will work.
He will work.
She will work.
We will work.
They will work.
She will travel tomorrow.
They will study next week.
I will read tonight.
He will write a message.

Lesson 4

🚫 Negative Sentences

النفي يختلف حسب الزمن. لاحظي أن الفعل يعود إلى base form بعد do / does / did.

Present Simple

I do not work.
You do not work.
He does not work.
She does not work.
They do not work.
⭐ لماذا نقول: She does not work وليس: She does not works؟ لأن does حملت علامة الشخص، لذلك يعود الفعل إلى شكله الأساسي: work.
Past Simple

I did not work.
She did not work.
They did not go.
He did not eat.
بعد did not نستخدم الفعل الأساسي: did not go ✅
did not went ❌
Future

I will not work.
She will not travel.
They will not study.

Short form:
I won't work.
She won't travel.
Lesson 5

❓ Questions

نستخدم كلمات مساعدة مختلفة حسب الزمن.

Present Simple

Do you work?
Do they study?
Does she work?
Does he study?
مع Does يعود الفعل إلى شكله الأساسي: Does she work? ✅
Does she works? ❌
Past Simple

Did you work yesterday?
Did she study?
Did they go home?
Did he eat breakfast?
Future

Will you work tomorrow?
Will she travel next week?
Will they study tonight?
Quick Review

🧠 Remember These 6 Rules

1. I / You / We / They → base verb

2. He / She / It → s / es / ies

3. Past regular → usually -ed

4. Past irregular → special form

5. Future → will + base verb

6. After do / does / did / will → base verb
